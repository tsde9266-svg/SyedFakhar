About

Syed Fakhar Abbas is a photographer based in Doha, Qatar. His work spans portraiture, editorial, commercial campaigns and live events — images defined by directness, human presence, and an eye sharpened by years of working closely with people across cultures.

Of Pakistani nationality and fluent in Arabic, English, Hindi and Urdu, Syed brings a multilingual, multicultural perspective to every project. The portfolio presented here is a selection of work across multiple disciplines, submitted as evidence of creative practice and visual talent.
